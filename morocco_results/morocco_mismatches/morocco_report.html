Primer Mismatches Report

22/08/2026

Overview

This report summarises primer mismatches identified across 68 primers evaluated against 5 sequences from Morocco. Potentially problematic primers shown in the mismatch heatmap (Figure 1) were flagged based on the default 3′ and SNP/indel thresholds (≥1 3′ mismatches or ≥4 SNP/indel mismatches) adapted from Huang et al. (2024) and Bru et al. (2008). These primers may affect the sensitivity, specificity, and amplification efficiency of PCR.
